## A stronger, narrow promise

**One rich brief starts a personal world you can keep updating.**

Make the brief supply identity, actual work, audience, world preference, useful visitor action and editing constraints. The skill chooses a coherent original direction, builds within the existing stack, checks the result and records how to maintain it. A single initiating prompt can trigger many agent steps and revisions. Missing material facts may still require clarification; model/tool availability affects what can be completed. Do not promise instantaneous, unattended, production-ready results.

Our proposed distinction is the full relationship: **a work entry becomes a meaningful world object and an ordinary readable entry; an edit reconciles both while preserving unrelated placements**. That is narrower and easier to prove than being the most creative design skill. It is a product hypothesis, not a verified first invention or a shipped guarantee.

## Modularity that enables creativity

Keep independent decisions small:

- **Content:** identity, stable entry IDs, publication and supplied relations.
- **World grammar:** sea, mountains, space, city or nature; each owns composition and its vocabulary of objects.
- **Art direction:** silhouette, materials, light, camera and deliberate atmosphere.
- **Interaction:** an inspection lens, section view, docking or unfolding mechanism tied to content.
- **Host/adapters:** the existing app, editable content source, rendering limits and readable fallback.

Not every combination is coherent. A recipe should recommend compatible choices and reject combinations that weaken its story. Start with two contrasting implemented worlds, then earn further modules through real adaptations.

Futuristic work can use responsive optics, layered material reveals or a camera that shifts between landscape and information. Each should expose supplied work or relationships, remain legible and have a keyboard/mobile fallback. Added bloom, particles and HUD labels alone do not create a distinct experience.

## What to demonstrate and reuse

Publish one authentic brief → recognized skill invocation → visibly distinct result → direct readable entry → successful edit, stating time compression and human revisions. Compare two briefs to show that changing the person changes the composition and interaction, rather than only colors.

Use general organizational lessons through new authored guidance. This pass copied no code, prose, models or media. ORBIT's [license](https://github.com/HenrikBrehm/orbit/blob/main/LICENSE) restricts reuse; Memory Palace states separate permission requirements for its inner app. Any later asset/code reuse needs its own applicable terms and notices; comparison alone provides no clearance.
