This is an edited demonstration, not a native 24 fps screen recording, a performance benchmark, or a generated depiction of unfinished features. No interpolated UI frames were synthesized.

Title cards and captions were authored for this video. The introductory prompt is an example of invoking the portable skill; it is not footage of an agent executing that prompt or a fourth shipped world. All three demonstrated settings use the shared destination controls, rather than free steering or a collision simulation.

The edit contains the starter's illustrative content and project-authored procedural scenes. It includes no source-site portrait, paper figures, private CMS records, stock footage, reference-site imagery, or music. Installed system fonts rendered the captions; font software is not bundled. Three.js retains its separate MIT notice in the repository and every starter build. The video and derivatives are recorded in [the asset ledger](../data/assets.json).

Encoding checks verify H.264, `yuv420p`, 1280 × 720, 24 fps, a 45-second duration and no audio stream. A contact sheet and decoded stills were inspected. Temporary raw capture frames and editing work files are outside the release; this repository does not include browser/session archives.
