# Interaction refinement — 8 October 2026

This is a local refinement after the earlier GitHub and Hugging Face release.
The complaint was that the world settings survived, but the more meaningful
Aegean interaction did not lead the experience or the film.

## What changed

- A published project or study with supplied media can be selected explicitly.
  Its collection landmark opens that selected work; other entries remain in
  the ordinary index. The project example now supplies three original diagrams
  explaining Kaimerva, distinct from the illustrative research study.
- One source-image carrier has three authored housings: the sea optical iris,
  orbital gantry with folding/retracting shutters and moving scan carriage,
  and woodland timber/glass cabinet with opening doors and stage drawers.
- The native reader can change the setting while retaining the selected
  record, source view, explanations and visited-view state. The new instrument
  loads the same source. Late image loads from replaced instruments cannot
  overwrite the active one.
- Inspection is placed at the selected collection. Return in the same setting
  restores its saved exploration pose; a setting change returns to the new
  setting's atlas. Focus resolves by the invoking landmark's stable destination
  even after the old landmark DOM has been replaced.
- All fifteen authored landmark sets are pickable, including the observatory
  telescope. Selection tests bounded actual meshes, with the central sea field
  instrument retaining its existing action. A small tap activates the same
  destination as its HTML control; movement, cancellation or lost interaction
  state cancels that tap. Terrain, travelers and unrelated decorations are
  excluded from landmark triangle testing.
- Moving an active entry into a different collection during content refresh
  closes inspection into its new readable index, rather than keeping an obsolete
  scene anchor. Reordering views preserves a still-valid stage ID.
- The portable skill carries the tested, byte-identical instrument module and
  its MIT notice. Its integration guide separates host responsibilities from
  the renderer. The module supplies no camera, reader, router or CMS.

## Boundaries

Only sea, orbital and woodland housings exist. Mountains, city workshops,
comparison instruments and richer journal/timeline/news interactions remain
design proposals. The starter still has five collection landmarks, rather than
an automatic scene object for each new record. It has destination navigation,
not free sailing physics. Existing films use earlier footage and do not show
this refinement.

The prior novelty comparison is a dated comparison of the earlier guidance.
It is not a global uniqueness claim or fresh third-party code comparison for
this new module. The new meshes, diagrams and controller integration were
authored in this repository; Three.js remains the declared MIT dependency.
